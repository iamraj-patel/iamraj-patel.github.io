06 — FAQ

QUESTIONS
I ACTUALLY GET

01.Raj, are you a Windows guy or a Linux guy?

Both, and that’s the point. Most environments are mixed, and the interesting failures happen exactly where the two meet.

02.Do you actually run Kubernetes at home?

Yes. kubeadm clusters with containerd and Calico, rebuilt often on purpose. Breaking a lab cluster is much cheaper than learning on production.

03.Why so much automation?

Because manual work does not scale, does not audit well, and never behaves the same way at 3am. Automation is documentation that executes.

04.What do you do when something breaks?

Reproduce, isolate, follow the dependency chain, fix the cause rather than the symptom, then write it down so the next person spends ten minutes instead of two hours.

05.Where is your résumé and phone number?

Deliberately not on this site. Email works perfectly well, and anything else can follow from there.
